For informational purposes only. This is a free reference tool that supports a qualified professional's judgment. It is not legal, medical, engineering or compliance advice, and using it does not make OEMS anyone's consultant.

  1. Results depend on your inputs. The tool assumes an accurate 8-hour TWA from calibrated, correctly set-up instruments, an accurate protector NRR, and, if used, a valid fit-test PAR. It does not account for impulse or peak noise, measurement uncertainty, chemicals that can damage hearing (ototoxicants), noise exposure outside work, or how much a protector actually blocks for a given worker. Real-world protection depends on fit, condition and wearing the protector the entire time in noise. The NIOSH derating is a general estimate, not a measurement for any one person. The tool does not determine whether a worker has a standard threshold shift. That determination comes from audiometric testing reviewed by a qualified professional.
  2. Not a compliance determination. A PASS result does not mean a workplace complies with OSHA. A hearing conservation program also requires monitoring, audiometric testing, training and recordkeeping.
